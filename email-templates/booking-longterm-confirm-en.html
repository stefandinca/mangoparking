📍 How to find us

Radarului 1, Corbeanca, Ilfov — 5 minutes from Henri Coandă Airport

📍 Open in Google Maps

Our recommendation:

Plan your arrival 2–3 hours before your flight and allow an extra 20 minutes for our service. Even though we are only 5 minutes from the airport, some periods — weekends and holiday seasons — can be busier than usual.

Arrival process

1. 🚗 Enter the parking area. A team member will welcome you at the entrance, and your luggage goes directly into the Mangobus.

2. 🅿️ Park your car — we will guide you to your spot, or park it for you if you prefer.

3. ✈️ Head to the terminal. The Mangobus takes you directly to the airport terminal (approx. 5 minutes).

4. 🥭 We pick you up on return. When you land, call us from the departures terminal and we will come and collect you.

We take care of your car — the rest is your holiday. Have a great trip! ✈️
